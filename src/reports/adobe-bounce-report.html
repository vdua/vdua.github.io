Cross-cutting findings

  • Geo-location interstitial modals fire on first paint on the two mobile region pages (India CC, LATAM CC Pro) and obscure CTAs. These two pages also carry the highest bounce rates (79% and 96%). This is the single most actionable, repeatable bounce driver in the set.
  • Mobile dominates 3 of the top 5 pages (84%–100% mobile). Mobile experience quality is decisive for adobe.com's highest-traffic pages.
  • Two distinct "high bounce" causes exist and should not be treated the same: (a) blocking interstitials / slow load on acquisition pages (fixable), and (b) informational/notice pages where single-visit exits are expected (Genuine alert, tax resource).
  • Error rates vary wildly: Genuine alert 30%, homepage 19% — both deserve a follow-up error-clustering analysis to separate benign noise from broken functionality.
