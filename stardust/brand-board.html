Voice

A thoughtful engineer sharing notes from the workbench. Clear, specific, honest about what they know and what they're still figuring out.

Clear Specific Honest Curious Grounded Not: Hype-driven Not: Jargon-heavy Not: Condescending Not: Vague
Do
"Here's what I learned building this, and where I got stuck."
Opens with vulnerability — builds trust
"The architecture looks simple on paper. In practice, three things broke."
Contrast between theory and practice — shows real experience
"I'm not sure this is the right approach, but here's my reasoning."
Intellectual honesty — invites discussion
Don't
"This revolutionary AI paradigm will disrupt everything!"
Hype without substance
"Obviously, you should use microservices here."
Condescending assumption
"In this blog post, I will discuss..."
Academic throat-clearing — just start

Hard Rules

  • Write like you're explaining to a smart colleague over coffee
  • Show your work — include code, configs, error messages
  • Admit uncertainty — "I think" and "in my experience" are fine
  • No em dashes — use commas, colons, or periods
  • No exclamation marks in technical prose
